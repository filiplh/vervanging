# Glossary

The words vervanging uses for its own things. One term per thing; use it in code, docs, issues and copy. Changes come
through the plan PRs. On-screen words are Dutch; the code name is what identifiers use.

| Term (nl, on screen) | Code name | Means | Avoid | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| basisvijf | `startingFive` | The five players the coach picks to start the match; the plan is built from them. | starters, beginploeg | [grill D20](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) (term: agent's call) |
| blok | `block` | A fixed stretch of playing time with one line-up: 3:20 in the equal schedule, 5:00 in the 5-minute schedule. Minutes are counted in blocks, never measured. | periode (that is a quarter), shift, stint | [grill D4, D6](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) |
| in the zone | `inTheZone` | Keeping a player who is due off on court for the next block; the player who missed the block gets back the zone player's next planned block where they are not already on; with none left, the difference stays and is shown (+1/−1 block). | blijven staan, extra blok | [grill D8](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) |
| extra minuten | `extraMinutes` | The in-the-zone variant where the kept player does not give the block back: they play it on top, and the blocks left are spread over the others. Chosen on purpose, rarely; the minutes show the difference. | beloning, bonus | [phase 2 D2, D6](grill/2026-11-22-phase-2.md#decisions) |
| schema | `schedule` | How a match is split into blocks: "gelijke minuten" (12 × 3:20) or "blokken van 5 minuten" (8 × 5:00). | plan (that is the grid of who plays which block) | [grill D6](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) |
| uit / terug | `out` / `back` | Marking a player unavailable for the rest of the match (fouls, injury), or available again; the remaining blocks are re-spread. | geblesseerd, vijf fouten (reasons, not states) | [grill D11](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) |
| wissel | `sub` | The moment between two blocks when players go off and on, at the first dead ball after a block ends; also the button that moves to the next block. | vervanging (the app's name), switch | [grill D12, D21](grill/2026-11-22-whole-product.md#decisions) |
