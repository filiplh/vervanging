# Phase 3 exit: every screen on the three screens

Screenshots for the phase 3 exit test (plan decision #41, issue #107): Spelers, setup, live and the end screen on the
iPhone 17 Pro and on both iPhone Duo screens, light and dark. The files are in
[`2026-phase-3/`](2026-phase-3/), named `<screen>-<device>-<light|dark>.png`.

> **These are simulator screenshots, not a real device.** No real phone was used. ⚠ How the app looks and feels on a
> real iPhone 17 Pro or a real Duo (glass in a bright hall, touch targets under the thumb, the real unfolded inner
> screen) is not shown here and is not confirmed.

## How they were made

- Build: the Debug build of `main` at `8d92a87` (after the last phase 3 screen ticket, #159), Xcode 27.2, iOS 27.2
  simulators, minimum iOS 27.0. Picture-taking only: a throw-away UI test kept outside the repo (nothing of it is
  committed) filled the roster, tapped through the screens and saved `XCUIScreen` screenshots. No app or test code
  in the repo was changed.
- Data: a roster of 12 players with first names only, in a scratch folder (`VERVANGING_STORAGE_DIR`), and the fixed
  seed 7 (`VERVANGING_SEED`), so the owner's simulator data was never touched: #4 Lotte, #5 Marie-Charlotte
  Anne-Sophie (the long name, cut with … on setup and live), #7 Fien, #8 Mila, #10 Noor, #11 Lien, #12 Anna, #13
  Elise, #14 Hanne, #15 Jade, #21 Lore, #23 Marie. The names are made up.
- The match uses the setup's default plan for 12 players (12 blocks of 3:20), played through to Einde match with one
  uit/terug and one In the zone along the way; every player ends on 16:40.
- The simulator's status bar was set to 9:41 with full battery and signal (`simctl status_bar`), so the pictures look
  the same; the app's own screens are untouched. Light and dark are the simulator's appearance setting.
- Sizes: each picture was scaled down to at most 1300 px on its long side; the inner-screen rows were rotated upright
  and cropped as described above.
- Appearance of each picture: the tables show Licht (light) first, then Donker (dark). Click a picture for the full file.

## What was seen

Only what the pictures show; nothing here is a judgment on how the screens work for a coach.

- Picture sizes before scaling: the iPhone 17 Pro is 1206 × 2622 px, the Duo's outer screen 1398 × 2034 px (wider and
  shorter).
- The long name #5 Marie-Charlotte Anne-Sophie is cut with … on the setup, in Bij de volgende wissel, in the minutes
  table and in the wide live screen. It shows whole on every end screen in these pictures.
- On the iPhone 17 Pro the end list fits above the Nieuwe match bar. On the Duo's outer screen the last two rows (#21,
  #23) sit under the bar until the list is scrolled (the two end pictures). In the turned inner-screen stand-in the
  same two rows sit under the bar too; ⚠ that picture was not scrolled, so whether the list scrolls there is not
  shown here.
- The live screen in the two inner-screen stand-ins shows the court and the whole minutes table without scrolling,
  also with In the zone and with a player uit.
